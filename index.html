At Greg's Banh Mi, we're serving up the perfect marriage of French and Vietnamese culinary traditions—crispy-on-the-outside, pillowy-on-the-inside baguettes loaded with bold, fresh flavours that'll make your taste buds sing.

Classic Pork Banh Mi

Tender marinated pork topped with crisp pickled vegetables, fresh cilantro, and a smear of pâté on a perfectly crusty baguette.

Lemongrass Chicken Banh Mi

Grilled chicken marinated in fragrant lemongrass and garlic, paired with cucumber, jalapeños, and our house mayo.

Tofu Banh Mi

Crispy pan-fried tofu glazed in sweet soy sauce, accompanied by fresh cucumbers and pickled daikon.

Special Banh Mi

A house special blend of savory cold cuts, rich pâté, and a generous mix of fresh herbs.
